# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who dictate substantial text on a Mac and need accurate words to appear continuously and reliably in the focused editor. Speed, privacy, unobtrusive controls, and recoverability matter.

## Product Purpose

Cadence provides fast, accurate, privacy-conscious macOS dictation by transcribing locally and inserting completed words live through the editor's standard paste path. Success means speech stays visibly in flow, pauses finalize the last words and punctuation without ending the session, and the transcript is saved or recoverable.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and dependable. Cadence should feel like a native Mac utility with a small moment of recognizable character, not a web product transplanted into a desktop shell.

## Anti-references

Avoid bulky always-visible overlays, decorative dashboards, excessive status copy, attention-seeking motion, generic AI branding, and controls that fight standard macOS focus or window behavior. Reference the compactness and immediacy of leading dictation utilities without copying their identity.

## Design Principles

- Disappear when idle; become clear when active.
- Make the user-visible result the source of truth.
- Preserve flow by keeping primary interactions immediate and non-activating.
- Prefer familiar macOS behavior over novel interaction for its own sake.
- Make failure recoverable without losing the transcript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use meaningful accessibility labels, keyboard-accessible primary controls, sufficient contrast, and system-native interaction semantics. Respect reduced-motion preferences for nonessential transitions, keep collapsed controls discoverable with practical pointer targets, and never rely on color alone to communicate state.
